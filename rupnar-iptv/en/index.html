Features

Everything you expect from a good player

On PC, with a mouse or the keyboard alone; on phones and tablets, with a touch interface.

Multiple accounts

Xtream server (username and password) or M3U playlist from a URL or file. Save several and switch between them instantly.

Live TV with preview

Watch a channel in a small window next to its programme guide, then go full size without interruption. Reconnects automatically if the signal drops.

Movies and series

Posters, full details, season picker and automatic playback of the next episode.

Continue watching

Pick up movies, episodes and channels right where you left off, straight from the menu.

Favourites, search and filter

Mark favourites, search within each category and hide the categories you don't care about.

Audio and subtitles

Choose the audio and subtitle language; the app remembers your preference for the next videos.

Full-featured player

Playback speed, picture-in-picture, full screen, "Go to live" and, on PC, keyboard shortcuts.

Many formats

Also plays video and audio that browsers can't handle (AVI, MPEG-2, Dolby…), automatically.

Private and always up to date

Your login details are stored encrypted on your device. No accounts, no tracking. Updates itself.

Live TV

Preview and programme guide at a glance

  • One click: the channel plays small, with its upcoming programmes.
  • Another click: full size, with no cuts or reconnections.
  • Fallen behind? "Go to live" brings you back to now.
  • Respects your account: never opens two connections at once.
Movies and series

Everything in order, nothing to hunt for

  • Details with plot, cast, year, running time and rating.
  • Season picker, with watched episodes marked.
  • When an episode ends, the next one starts.
  • Your progress is saved automatically, for each account.
Player

Full controls, on PC and on mobile

  • Audio and subtitles, speed from 0.5× to 2×.
  • Picture-in-picture and full screen.
  • 10-second skips, previous and next episode.
  • On mobile: tap to show the controls and double-tap either side to skip 10 s.
  • On PC, shortcuts: Space, arrows, F, M, P, A, C, F11…
Your way

Keep only what interests you

  • Hide categories with one click; new ones appear on their own.
  • Starred favourites, always at hand.
  • Search that ignores accents, capitals and word order.

Windows: SmartScreen warning

The first time, Windows may show "Windows protected your PC". This is normal: the installer doesn't have a paid digital signature yet, so Windows doesn't recognise it yet. To install:

  1. Open RupnarIPTV-Setup.exe.
  2. On the blue warning, click "More info".
  3. Click "Run anyway".
  4. Installation finishes on its own and the app opens.

If your browser says the file "isn't commonly downloaded", choose ··· → Keep. Only download it from this website or from the official repository.

Everything loaded in the app (channels, categories, movies, series and images) comes from a third-party IPTV playlist or service whose login details you enter yourself.

FAQ

Common questions

Does the app include channels or playlists?

No. Rupnar IPTV is only a player: you need your own IPTV service (Xtream server or M3U playlist) provided by a third party.

What do I need to get started?

Your service's login details: the server URL with your username and password (Xtream), or the address or file of your M3U playlist.

Is it free?

Yes, completely: no ads, no subscriptions and no paid features. Donations are voluntary.

Where are my passwords stored?

Only on your device, encrypted: on Windows, with the operating system's own protection; on Android, with Android Keystore. They are never sent anywhere except the IPTV server you specify. More details in the privacy policy.

Why does Windows show a warning when installing?

Because the installer isn't digitally signed yet. Click "More info" and then "Run anyway". See the installation steps.

How does it update?

On its own. On Windows, it checks for a new version when it opens and installs it automatically; on Android, it updates through Google Play.

Is there a version for TVs?

Samsung TV and Android TV / Google TV versions are on the way. The Google Play version is for phones and tablets.
